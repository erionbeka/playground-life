# Business Associate Agreement (Template)

> **Template only — review by qualified counsel is required before use.**

This Business Associate Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **[Covered Entity — Clinic]** ("Covered Entity") and **[Playground Life Vendor Legal Entity]** ("Business Associate") effective as of ____.

## 1. Services
Business Associate provides the Playground Life platform (hosting, support, and maintenance), which stores or transmits Protected Health Information ("PHI") on behalf of Covered Entity, including child identifiers, clinical notes, and therapy session analytics.

## 2. Permitted uses
Business Associate may use PHI solely to deliver and support the Services, including: hosting, backups, security monitoring, and de-identified aggregate analytics. Sale of PHI and use for marketing are prohibited.

## 3. Safeguards
Business Associate shall: (a) encrypt PHI in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest; (b) enforce unique user access with MFA for workforce accounts; (c) maintain audit logs; (d) apply the retention schedule agreed in Exhibit A; (e) restrict workforce access per minimum-necessary standard.

## 4. Subcontractors
Subcontractors that create, receive, or transmit PHI must sign agreements with terms no less protective. Current subprocessors are listed in Exhibit B; Covered Entity approval required for additions.

## 5. Incident reporting
Business Associate shall notify Covered Entity within **24 hours** of discovering a suspected breach of unsecured PHI and cooperate through investigation and notification obligations.

## 6. Access & amendment
Upon Covered Entity request, Business Associate shall provide access to, amend, or delete PHI as required by 45 CFR §164.524/526/528.

## 7. Accounting of disclosures
Business Associate shall record disclosures and make records available within 10 business days.

## 8. Termination
Either party may terminate on 30 days' notice if a material breach is uncured. Upon termination, Business Associate shall return, then destroy, all PHI (certifying destruction) unless legally prohibited.
