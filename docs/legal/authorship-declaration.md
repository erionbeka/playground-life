## Legal basis (Republic of Kosovo)

This declaration is prepared by reference to **Law No. 08/L-205 on Copyright
and Related Rights**, published in the Official Gazette of the Republic of
Kosovo No. **22/2023** on **12 October 2023** and in force from **27 October
2023** (Art. 147: fifteen days after publication). That law repealed Law No.
04/L-065 (2011), Law No. 05/L-047 (2016) and Law No. 06/L-120 (2019)
(Art. 146).

Key provisions relied on:

- **Art. 7** — "The author is the natural person who created the work."
- **Art. 8** — Presumption of authorship: the person whose name ordinarily
  appears on the work is presumed to be the author until proved otherwise.
- **Art. 15** — Copyright protection is **automatic**: the author enjoys
  protection by the mere fact of creating the work, and **no registration or
  other formality is required** for its existence or enforcement.
- **Art. 16** — Protection is available to authors who are nationals of, or
  resident in, the Republic of Kosovo.
- **Art. 19 & 20** — Moral rights of authorship (to be recognised and named as
  author) and integrity subsist even after any transfer or licence of the
  author's economic rights.
- **Art. 22(1)** — Moral rights **cannot** be transferred or licensed.
- **Art. 64** — The moral rights under Arts. 18, 19 and 20 are protected
  **without limitation in time**; the right of regret (Art. 21) subsists only
  during the author's life.
- **Art. 65(1)** — Economic rights last for the author's life plus **70 years**
  after death, counted from 1 January of the year following death.

### Note on formality

Under Art. 15 of Law No. 08/L-205, no registration is needed for copyright to
exist or be enforced. This signed declaration serves as **evidence** of
authorship and provenance (reinforcing the Art. 8 presumption). A person may
optionally lodge a dated copy with a notary or a trusted timestamping service
to strengthen its evidentiary value.
