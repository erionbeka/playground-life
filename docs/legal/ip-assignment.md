## Background

This Agreement is made under **Law No. 08/L-205 on Copyright and Related
Rights** (Official Gazette No. 22/2023, 12 October 2023, in force 27 October
2023) ("the Law").

(A) The Assignee created the software and documentation known as **"Playground
Life"** (the "Work").

(B) The Assignor **did not create or contribute to the Work**. To the extent
the Assignor owns, or may be regarded as owning or claiming, any copyright or
related right in the Work, the Assignor wishes to assign and confirm the
transfer of all such economic rights to the Assignee, so that the Assignee is
and remains the sole owner.

(C) Under **Art. 37(1)** of the Law, authors and other owners may transfer or
assign their economic rights by contract, and **as a result of such a
transfer the assignee becomes the new owner of the copyright**.

## 1. Assignment

1.1 The Assignor hereby **assigns to the Assignee**, absolutely and with
effect from the creation of the Work, all of the Assignor's right, title and
interest (if any) in and to the Work, including:

- all **economic rights** within the meaning of Art. 23 of the Law (repro­duction,
  distribution, rental, public performance, broadcasting, communication to the
  public, making available, translation, adaptation), and all rights to
  remuneration;
- all copyright and all other intellectual property rights of any kind; and
- all rights of action, claims and remedies in respect of any past, present or
  future infringement.

1.2 As a result of this assignment, the **Assignee is the new and sole owner**
of the rights assigned (Art. 37(1)).

## 2. Scope, term and territory (required by Art. 38)

1. The parties specify the mandatory content of a copyright contract under
   **Art. 38(1)** as follows:
   - **Nature and extent of use:** all forms of exploitation of the Work and
     the right to authorise or prohibit use by others.
   - **Validity period:** the **entire legal term** of copyright protection
     (the author's life plus 70 years, Art. 65(1)), and in any event for the
     **longest period permitted by applicable law**.
   - **Territory:** **worldwide**.
   - **Remuneration:** **[AMOUNT, e.g. EUR 1 / or "for good and valuable
     consideration"]**, the receipt of which the Assignor acknowledges. Under
     **Art. 41(2)** a lump sum may be agreed where a percentage basis is
     impractical.
   - **Payment conditions and deadlines:** **[e.g. paid on signature]**.

2. The parties have deliberately specified term and territory. If they had not,
   the Law would imply a limited scope: absence of a stated territory limits
   the contract to uses **in the Republic of Kosovo** (Art. 38(2)), and absence
   of a stated term limits it to **3 years** (original form) or **5 years**
   (adapted/translated form) (Art. 38(3)).

## 3. Existing works only (Art. 39)

This assignment covers only the **existing, identified Work** described above.
It does **not** purport to assign or authorise any right in an indefinite
number of as-yet uncreated future works, which under **Art. 39(1)** would be
invalid.

## 4. Moral rights (Arts. 19, 20, 22)

4.1 The Assignor acknowledges that, under **Art. 22(1)** of the Law, **moral
rights cannot be transferred or licensed**. The parties do not attempt to
transfer them. The Assignee's moral rights, including the right to be
recognised and named as author (Art. 19) and the right of integrity (Art. 20),
are protected without limitation in time (Art. 64).

4.2 To the extent the Assignor holds any moral rights in the Work, the
Assignor (i) waives the right of regret under Art. 21 to the fullest extent
the Law allows, and (ii) consents, for the purposes of **Art. 22(2)**, to the
Assignee's use, reproduction, modification and exploitation of the Work.

## 5. Further assurances

The Assignor shall, at the Assignee's request and expense, promptly execute
any further documents and do everything reasonably necessary to give full
effect to this Agreement and to **evidence or record the transfer** of the
rights assigned.

## 6. Warranties

The Assignor warrants and represents that:

1. the Assignor has not created or contributed to the Work, and to the best of
   the Assignor's knowledge no right of the Assignor subsists in it;
2. to the extent any such right does subsist, the Assignor owns it and has not
   assigned, licensed or encumbered it;
3. the Assignor has disclosed in writing any matter within the Assignor's
   knowledge concerning third-party material or material generated by
   artificial intelligence; and
4. the Assignor has full capacity and authority to enter into this Agreement.

## 7. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the **laws of the Republic of Kosovo**. The
parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the **competent courts in
Prishtina, Republic of Kosovo**. Supplementary matters not regulated by the
Law are governed by the applicable Kosovo law on obligations (Art. 40(7) of
the Law).
