## INTRODUCTION: WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS AND IS NOT

This is a **clarifying agreement**. It does not create a new project, it does not incorporate a company, and it does not assert any claim against anyone. Its purpose is simple and sincere: to record in writing, calmly and respectfully, what the Parties already know — that **the rights in the software work "Playground Life" belong to the Author**, who created the work himself.

This document does not seek to deny the value of the Parties' shared experience at the hackathon, does not aim to diminish anyone's participation, and contains no blame. On the contrary, it acknowledges with gratitude the shared experience and each person's effort in that context, while clarifying, precisely, who created the work and to whom its rights belong.

This document is **not** a demand for money, it is not a lawsuit, and it is not a profit-sharing agreement. It takes away no one's friendship, respect or good memories of work done together; it deals only with the legal rights in the Work, as is required for legal certainty and to protect the future of the Work.

## PREAMBLE AND SPIRIT OF THE DOCUMENT

This Agreement is made in good faith, in the spirit of honesty, mutual respect and the preservation of good relations between the people who cooperated around the **pristina.innovate.healthcare** hackathon.

The purpose of this document is **not** to create disputes, criticism or distance between the Parties. On the contrary, its purpose is to clarify, fairly and courteously, what all the Parties already know about the rights in the Work, so that no one is left uncertain about the future and so that the Parties' friendship and cooperation remain intact. This is a document of clarification and gratitude, not of accusation.

The Parties make this Agreement on the conviction that **stating the truth plainly is an act of respect**, that good relationships are strengthened when rights are clear, and that silent ambiguities are what, over time, spoil friendships. This document is therefore, in essence, an act of care for the shared future and for the Work.
