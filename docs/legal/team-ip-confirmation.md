## RECITALS

**A.** The Author has created, alone, the software, documentation, design,
code, interfaces and assets known as **"Playground Life"** (together, the
"Work").

**B.** The initial idea was presented at the **pristina.innovate.healthcare**
hackathon (04–06 April 2025, Prishtina, organised by HealthCare Futurists
GmbH).

**C.** At that event **only a slide presentation and a few small pictures** of
how the app might look were presented. **The application was not submitted** to
the hackathon, and no working code, repository or build of the application was
handed over to the organiser or anyone else at that event. The output of the
event was **presentation material only** (slides and images), not software.

**D.** The present codebase and the Work in its current form were **created
after the event**, beginning in **April 2026** — about one year later — and are
**not a hackathon submission**.

**E.** Under **Art. 7** of the Law, the author is the **natural person who
created the work**. The Author created the Work. The Confirming Parties did not
create the Work and made no creative contribution to it.

**F.** The Parties confirm that, under **Art. 15** of the Law, copyright arises
**automatically on creation** and **no registration** or other formality is
required for its existence or enforcement.

**G.** The purpose of this Agreement is to remove all doubt and prevent any
misunderstanding about title to the Work, by confirming clearly and
irrevocably that **the Author is the sole owner** of the Work.

Accordingly, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. CONFIRMATION OF FACT

1.1 Each Confirming Party confirms that, to the best of their knowledge, they
did **not create** the Work and are **not its author or co-author** within the
meaning of Arts. 7 and 9 of the Law.

1.2 Any specific contribution a Confirming Party believes they made must be
described in writing on their signature line. A line left blank is confirmed as
**"none"**.

1.3 Without limitation, the following do **not** constitute a creative
contribution and create no right in the Work: team, group or hackathon
participation; discussing the idea or giving opinions, comments or suggestions;
attending meetings, chats, presentations or group activities; presenting
slides or sharing pictures or materials prepared by the Author; testing,
reviewing, commenting on or using the Work; logistical, administrative,
financial, motivational or organisational tasks; the general idea, concept or
theme (which is not protected as such by copyright); and any oral, informal or
undocumented involvement.

1.4 Each Confirming Party confirms that they were **not engaged** by the Author
(or anyone else) as employee, employer, contractor, consultant or co-author in
relation to the Work, and were **not paid** and promised no payment or
remuneration for the Work, except as stated on their signature line.

1.5 Each Confirming Party confirms that the Author is the **sole person** who
created the Work, and that the Confirming Party has **no right, title or
interest** in the Work or any part of it.

1.6 Each Confirming Party confirms that they do not hold, and have not kept,
any copy or copy, clone, branch or material of the Work not lawfully made
available to them by the Author.

## 2. CONFIRMATORY ASSIGNMENT

2.1 To the extent — and only to the extent — that any Confirming Party owns or
may be regarded as owning any right, title or interest in the Work, that
Confirming Party hereby **assigns to the Author**, absolutely and
unconditionally, with effect from the creation of the Work, all of their right,
title and interest in the Work, including all **economic rights** under Art. 23
of the Law, all rights to remuneration, and all claims and remedies.

2.2 As a result of this assignment, **the Author is the new and sole owner** of
the rights assigned (Art. 37(1)).

2.3 The Parties specify the content required by **Art. 38(1)** of the Law as
follows: **nature and extent of use** — all forms of exploitation of the Work
and the right to authorise or prohibit use by others; **validity period** —
the entire legal term of copyright protection (the author's life plus **70
years**, Art. 65(1)), and in any event the longest period permitted by law;
**territory** — worldwide; and **remuneration** — none (see clause 9).

2.4 The Parties deliberately choose the broadest lawful scope. Had they left
these matters unstated, the Law would imply a term of only 3 years (original
form) or 5 years (adapted form) (Art. 38(3)) and a territory limited to the
**Republic of Kosovo** only (Art. 38(2)).

## 3. EXISTING WORKS ONLY

This Agreement covers only the **existing, identified Work**. It does not
purport to assign or authorise any right in an indefinite number of
as-yet-uncreated future works, which under **Art. 39(1)** of the Law would be
null and void.

## 4. MORAL RIGHTS

4.1 The Parties acknowledge that under **Art. 22(1)** of the Law moral rights
**cannot be transferred or licensed**, and this Agreement does not attempt to
do so.

4.2 To the extent any Confirming Party holds or claims any moral right in the
Work, that Confirming Party: (a) **waives**, to the fullest extent permitted by
the Law, the right of regret under Art. 21 and any moral-right claim in respect
of the Work; and (b) **consents**, for the purposes of Art. 22(2), to the
Author's use, reproduction, modification, distribution and exploitation of the
Work.

4.3 The Author's own moral rights — the right of first publication (Art. 18),
the right to be recognised and named as the author (Art. 19), and the right of
integrity (Art. 20) — remain his and are protected **without limitation in
time** (Art. 64).

## 5. COVENANT NOT TO ASSERT

Each Confirming Party covenants not to assert, and not to assist any other
person to assert, against the Author, his successors, licensees or customers,
any claim of ownership, authorship, moral right or compensation in respect of
the Work.

## 6. NO ADMISSION OF WRONGDOING

This Agreement is not an admission by any Party of any wrongdoing. It is a
good-faith record of facts and a settlement of any present or potential doubt
about ownership.

## 7. WARRANTIES

Each Confirming Party warrants that: (a) the facts they confirm in clause 1 are
true to the best of their knowledge; (b) if they contribute, the contribution
is their own original work, or they hold all rights necessary to assign it;
(c) they have not previously assigned, licensed or encumbered any right
purported to be assigned here; (d) they have disclosed in writing any
third-party or AI-generated material relating to the Work of which they are
aware; (e) they have full capacity and authority to enter into this Agreement;
and (f) they are under no contractual, employment or legal obligation that
prevents them from doing so.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY, THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURE AND AI TOOLS

8.1 Each Party shall keep confidential the contents of this Agreement and any
non-public information concerning the Work (the "**Confidential
Information**"), and shall not disclose it to any third party without the
Author's prior written consent.

8.2 Without limiting clause 8.1, **disclosing or uploading the Confidential
Information to an external, third-party artificial-intelligence,
cloud-computing or data-processing service that is not under the disclosing
Party's sole control is a disclosure to a third party** for the purpose of
clause 8.1 and requires the Author's prior written consent.

8.3 Clause 8.2 does **not** restrict disclosure: (a) to a Party's own legal,
tax, financial or other professional advisers who are under a duty of
confidentiality; (b) as required by law, a court or a competent authority; or
(c) of information that becomes public other than through a breach of this
Agreement. Nothing in this clause limits any Party's rights over their **own
personal data** under **Law No. 06/L-082 on Protection of Personal Data**.

8.4 A breach of this clause does **not** affect the validity or continuing
effect of the confirmations, assignment, waiver or covenants in this Agreement,
which remain in full force. The remedy for a breach of this clause is
**compensation for the loss proven to result from the breach**, and **not**
the loss or forfeiture of any right under this Agreement.

8.5 The Parties acknowledge that a Party who owns no right in the Work (see
clause 1) has **no right capable of being forfeited**, and that nothing in
this clause creates, transfers or confirms any ownership.

## 9. CONSIDERATION

As consideration for the confirmations, assignment, waiver and covenants in
this Agreement, the Author grants each Confirming Party the recognition stated
on their signature line (if any). The Parties acknowledge that no monetary
payment is required for the validity of this Agreement.

## 10. INDEMNITY

10.1 Each Confirming Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the Author against
any loss, damage, cost or third-party claim arising from (a) a breach of the
warranties in clause 7, or (b) any claim by that Party to rights in the Work
that is inconsistent with this Agreement.

10.2 This obligation survives the termination or expiry of this Agreement.

## 11. FURTHER ASSURANCES

Each Confirming Party shall, at the Author's request and expense, promptly
execute any further document reasonably necessary to give full effect to this
Agreement and to evidence, record or register the transfer of any rights.

This Agreement is governed by the **laws of the Republic of Kosovo**. The
Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the **competent courts in
Prishtina, Republic of Kosovo**. Supplementary matters not regulated by the Law

## 13. MISCELLANEOUS

13.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the
Parties on its subject matter and replaces any prior understanding.

13.2 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the rest remains in
force and the invalid provision is replaced by one as close as possible in

13.3 **Counterparts and electronic signature.** This Agreement may be signed in

13.4 **Notices.** Notices are sent to the Parties at the addresses stated in
