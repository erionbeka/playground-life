## RECITALS

**A.** The Author has created, alone, the software, documentation, design,
code, user interfaces and assets known as **"Playground Life"** (together, the
"Work").

**B.** The initial idea was presented at the **pristina.innovate.healthcare**
hackathon (04–06 April 2025, Prishtina, organised by HealthCare Futurists
GmbH).

**C.** At that event **only a slide presentation and a few small pictures** of
how the app might look were presented. **The application was not submitted** to
the hackathon, and no working code, repository or build of the application was
handed over to the organiser or to anyone else at that event. The output of the
event was **presentation material only** (slides and images), not software.

**D.** The present codebase and the Work in its current form were **created
after the event**, beginning in **April 2026** — about one year later — and are
**not a hackathon submission**.

**E.** Under **Art. 7** of the Law, the author is the **natural person who
created the work**; computer programs are protected works (Art. 5(2.2)). The
Author created the Work. The Confirming Parties did not create the Work.

**F.** Under **Art. 9** of the Law, a work of joint authorship is the result of
the **joint creative efforts** of two or more authors. No Confirming Party made
any such joint creative effort.

**G.** Under **Art. 15** of the Law, copyright arises **automatically on
creation** and **no registration** or other formality is required for its
existence or enforcement. The Author is protected as a resident of the Republic
of Kosovo (Art. 16).

**H.** The purpose of this Agreement is to remove all doubt and prevent any
misunderstanding about title to the Work, by confirming clearly and irrevocably
that **the Author is the sole owner** of the Work.

**I.** Each Party enters into this Agreement **voluntarily**, has had the
opportunity to obtain **independent legal advice** before signing, and signs
without any threat, duress, fraud or undue influence.

Accordingly, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Work"** means, without limitation and in any form or medium, all of the
following, whether in source form, object form, drawing or expressed idea:
source code, object code, scripts and any software component; software
architecture, models, diagrams and technical documentation; databases, data
schemas and configurations; user interfaces (UI/UX), graphic design, icons,
logos and visual style; texts, labels, content and user manuals; images,
photographs, sounds, animations and any other digital asset; algorithms,
business logic and embedded know-how; the presentation materials (slides) and
images prepared for or presented at the hackathon, to the extent they relate to
the Work; and every prior version, variant, copy and update of the foregoing.

1.2 **"Law"** means **Law No. 08/L-205 on Copyright and Related Rights**
(Official Gazette of the Republic of Kosovo No. 22/2023, 12 October 2023; in
force 27 October 2023), which repealed Law No. 04/L-065, Law No. 05/L-047 and
Law No. 06/L-120 (Art. 146 of the Law).

1.3 **"Obligations Law"** means **Law No. 04/L-077 on Obligational
Relationships** (Official Gazette No. 16/2012, 19 June 2012).

1.4 **"Rights"** means: (a) copyright; (b) moral rights; (c) the exclusive
economic rights under Art. 23 of the Law and all rights to remuneration;
(d) related rights, to the extent applicable; and (e) every other copyright,
neighbouring, sui generis or intellectual-property right, title or interest of
any kind, in any jurisdiction.

1.5 **"Confidential Information"** means the contents of this Agreement and any
non-public information concerning the Work.

## 2. CONFIRMATION OF FACT

2.1 Each Confirming Party confirms that, to the best of their knowledge, they
did **not create** the Work and are **not its author or co-author** within the
meaning of Arts. 7 and 9 of the Law.

2.2 Any specific contribution a Confirming Party believes they made must be
described in writing on their signature line. A line left blank is confirmed as
**"none"**.

2.3 Without limitation, the following do **not** constitute a creative
contribution and create no right in the Work: team, group or hackathon
participation; discussing the idea or giving opinions, comments or suggestions;
attending meetings, chats, presentations or group activities; presenting
slides, or sharing pictures or materials prepared by the Author; testing,
reviewing, commenting on or using the Work; logistical, administrative,
financial, motivational or organisational tasks; the general idea, concept or
theme (which is not protected as such by copyright); and any oral, informal or
undocumented involvement.

2.4 Each Confirming Party confirms that they were **not engaged** by the Author
(or by anyone else) as employee, employer, contractor, consultant, agent or
co-author in relation to the Work, and were **not paid**, promised, or
otherwise entitled to any payment or remuneration for the Work, except as
stated on their signature line.

2.5 Each Confirming Party confirms that the Author is the **only person** who
created the Work, and that the Confirming Party has **no right, title or
interest** in the Work or in any part of it.

2.6 Each Confirming Party confirms that they do **not possess** and have not
retained any copy, clone, branch, version or material of the Work that was not
lawfully made available to them by the Author.

## 3. CONFIRMATORY ASSIGNMENT

3.1 **Assignment.** To the extent — and only to the extent — that a Confirming
Party owns or may be regarded as owning any right, title or interest in the
Work, that Confirming Party **hereby assigns to the Author**, absolutely and
unconditionally, with effect from the creation of the Work, all of their right,
title and interest in the Work, including all economic rights under Art. 23 of
the Law, all rights to remuneration, and all present and future claims,
remedies and causes of action in respect of the Work.

3.2 **New owner.** As a result of this assignment, **the Author is the new and
sole owner** of the rights assigned (Art. 37(1)). This is a transfer of
**ownership** and not a mere licence.

3.3 **Mandatory content (Art. 38(1)).** The Parties specify: **nature and
extent of use** — all forms of exploitation of the Work known at the date of
this Agreement and the right to authorise or prohibit exploitation by others;
**duration** — the entire legal term of protection (the author's life plus
**70 years**, Art. 65(1)) and, in any event, the longest period permitted by
law; **territory** — worldwide; **remuneration** — see clause 10; and
**conditions and time of payment** — not applicable, as no remuneration is due.

3.4 **Broadest lawful scope.** The Parties deliberately choose the broadest
scope permitted by law. Had they left these matters unstated, the Law would
imply a term of only **3 years** (original form) or **5 years**
(adapted/translated form) (Art. 38(3)) and a territory limited to the
**Republic of Kosovo** only (Art. 38(2)).

3.4.1 The Parties confirm that, had any term of this Agreement been unclear,
**Art. 38(5)** of the Law would require the ambiguity to be resolved in favour
of the **author**. The Parties therefore record that they intend the broadest
lawful scope and that the provisions of this Agreement are to be read to give
the Author the fullest transfer permitted by law. Any ambiguity shall **not** be
construed against the Author.

## 4. EXISTING WORK ONLY; NO FUTURE WORKS OR UNKNOWN USES

4.1 **Existing work only.** This Agreement covers only the **existing,
identified Work**. It does not assign or authorise any right in an indefinite
number or unidentified purpose of as-yet-uncreated **future works**, which
under **Art. 39(1)** of the Law would be invalid.

4.2 **Unknown uses.** This Agreement does not purport to authorise, as a
separate unknown use, any method of exploitation that was **entirely unknown**
at the date of this Agreement. However, as permitted by **Art. 39(2)** of the
Law, each method of exploitation that is **already known** at that date, and
any method that **evolves from** a known method (including a method of the same
kind using new technical means), is included within the assignment of the
existing Work.

4.3 **Invalid clauses.** Any clause of this Agreement that is contrary to the
Law is **void**, and the provisions of the Law apply in its place (Art. 39(3)).

## 5. CO-AUTHOR WAIVER (SUBSIDIARY AND INDEPENDENT BASIS)

5.1 Without prejudice to clauses 2 and 3, and as a separate and independent
basis, if and to the extent a Confirming Party is, or is deemed to be, a
**co-author** of the Work, that Confirming Party **waives the exploitation of
its share of the rights**; and, by this waiver declaration, **its share passes
to the Author** as the only other author of the Work (Art. 9(4) of the Law).

## 6. MORAL RIGHTS

6.1 The Parties acknowledge that under **Art. 22(1)** of the Law moral rights
**cannot be transferred or licensed**, and this Agreement does not attempt to
do so.

6.2 To the extent a Confirming Party holds or claims any moral right in the
Work, that Confirming Party (a) **waives**, to the fullest extent permitted by
the Law, any moral-right claim over the Work, and (b) **consents**, for the
purposes of **Art. 22(2)** of the Law, to the Author's use, reproduction,
modification, distribution and exploitation of the Work. The Parties note that
the right of regret (Art. 21) is protected only during the author's life
(Art. 64(2)) and, in any event, does not apply to computer programs
(Art. 21(4)).

6.3 The Author's own moral rights — the right of first publication (Art. 18),
the right to be recognised and named as author (Art. 19), and the right of
integrity (Art. 20) — remain his and are protected **without limitation in
time** (Art. 64(1)).

7.1 Each Confirming Party covenants not to assert, and not to assist any other
person to assert, against the Author, his successors, assigns, licensees or
customers, any claim of ownership, authorship, co-authorship, moral right or
compensation in respect of the Work.

7.2 Each Confirming Party covenants not to contest any assertion by the Author
of ownership of the Work.

Each Confirming Party warrants that: (a) the facts they confirm in clause 2 are
true to the best of their knowledge; (b) if they contribute, the contribution

legal obligation** (including any employer or client claim) that would prevent

any third party; and (g) no third party holds any right in the Work derived
from them.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY, THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURE AND AI TOOLS

10.1 Each Party shall keep the **Confidential Information** confidential and
shall not disclose it to any third party without the Author's prior written
consent.

10.2 Without limiting clause 10.1, **disclosing or uploading the Confidential
Information to an external, third-party artificial-intelligence,
cloud-computing or data-processing service that is not under the disclosing
Party's sole control is a disclosure to a third party** for the purpose of
clause 10.1 and requires the Author's prior written consent.

10.3 Clause 10.2 does **not** restrict disclosure: (a) to a Party's own legal,
tax, financial or other professional advisers who are under a duty of
confidentiality; (b) as required by law, a court or a competent authority; or
(c) of information that becomes public other than through a breach of this
Agreement. Nothing in this clause limits any Party's rights over their **own
personal data** under **Law No. 06/L-082 on Protection of Personal Data**.

10.4 A breach of this clause does **not** affect the validity or continuing
effect of the confirmations, assignment, waiver or covenants in this Agreement,
which remain in full force. The remedy for a breach of this clause is
**compensation for the loss proven to result from the breach**, and **not** the
loss or forfeiture of any right under this Agreement.

10.5 The Parties acknowledge that a Party who owns no right in the Work (see
clause 2) has **no right capable of being forfeited**, and that nothing in this
clause creates, transfers or confirms any ownership.

17.4 **Counterparts and electronic signature.** This Agreement may be signed in
