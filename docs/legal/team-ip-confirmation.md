## Recitals

(A) Erion Beka created the software, documentation, designs and assets known as
**"Playground Life"** (the "Work").

(B) The Work originated as an idea at the **pristina.innovate.healthcare**
hackathon (04–06 April 2025). The present codebase was created **after** the
event, beginning **April 2026**, and is not a hackathon submission.

(C) The Parties acknowledge that, under **Law No. 08/L-205 on Copyright and
Related Rights** (Official Gazette No. 22/2023, 12 October 2023; in force 27
October 2023) (the "Law"): the author is the natural person who created the
work (Art. 7); copyright arises automatically on creation and requires no
registration (Art. 15); and joint authorship requires a **joint creative
effort** (Art. 9). A person who made no creative contribution is **not an
author** and holds **no copyright** in the Work.

(D) To remove all doubt, each Confirming Party confirms the facts about what
they did or did not do and, to the extent any right or claim of theirs exists
in the Work, assigns and waives it in favour of Erion Beka.

The Parties agree as follows.

## 1. Confirmation of facts

1.1 Each Confirming Party confirms that, to the best of their knowledge, they
did **not** create, write, design or contribute any part of the Work, except
as individually stated on their signature line below, and that Erion Beka is
the person who created the Work.

1.2 Each Confirming Party confirms that they were not engaged by Erion Beka (or
by anyone else) as an employee, contractor, consultant or co-author in respect
of the Work, and were not paid or promised anything for it, except as stated
on their signature line.

1.3 Any specific contribution a Confirming Party believes they made must be
described in writing on their signature line. A line left blank is confirmed
as **"none"**.

## 2. Confirmatory assignment

2.1 To the extent — and only to the extent — that any Confirming Party owns or
may be regarded as owning any copyright or related right in the Work, that
Confirming Party hereby **assigns to Erion Beka**, absolutely and with effect
from the creation of the Work, all of their right, title and interest in the
Work, including all **economic rights** under Art. 23 of the Law and all rights
to remuneration.

2.2 As a result of this assignment, **Erion Beka is the new and sole owner** of
the rights assigned (Art. 37(1)).

2.2.1 The Parties specify the content required by **Art. 38(1)** of the Law as
follows: **nature and extent of use** — all forms of exploitation of the Work;
**validity period** — the entire legal term of copyright protection (the
author's life plus 70 years, Art. 65(1)); **territory** — worldwide;
**remuneration** — none is required (see clause 8).

2.2 The Parties deliberately choose the broadest lawful scope. Had they left
these matters unstated, the Law would imply a term of only 3 years (original
form) or 5 years (adapted form) (Art. 38(3)) and a territory limited to the
**Republic of Kosovo** only (Art. 38(2)).

## 3. Existing works only

This Agreement covers only the **existing, identified Work**. It does not
purport to assign or authorise any right in an indefinite number of
as-yet-uncreated future works, which under **Art. 39(1)** of the Law would be
invalid.

## 4. Moral rights

4.1 The Parties acknowledge that under **Art. 22(1)** of the Law moral rights
**cannot be transferred or licensed**, and this Agreement does not attempt to
do so.

4.2 To the extent any Confirming Party holds or claims any moral right in the
Work, that Confirming Party (i) **waives**, to the fullest extent permitted by
the Law, the right of regret under Art. 21 and any moral-right claim in respect
of the Work, and (ii) **consents**, for the purposes of Art. 22(2), to Erion
Beka's use, reproduction, modification, distribution and exploitation of the
Work.

4.3 Erion Beka's own moral rights (to be recognised and named as the author,
and the right of integrity) remain his and are protected **without limitation
in time** (Art. 64).

## 5. Covenant not to assert

Each Confirming Party covenants not to assert, and not to assist any other
person to assert, against Erion Beka, his successors, licensees or customers,
any claim of ownership, authorship, moral right or compensation in respect of
the Work.

## 6. No admission of wrongdoing

This Agreement is not an admission by any Party of any wrongdoing. It is a
good-faith record of facts and a settlement of any present or potential doubt
about ownership.

## 7. Warranties

Each Confirming Party warrants that: (i) the facts they confirm in clause 1 are
true to the best of their knowledge; (ii) if they contribute, the contribution
is their own original work, or they hold all rights necessary to assign it;
(iii) they have disclosed any third-party or AI-generated material relating to
the Work that they are aware of; and (iv) they have full capacity and authority
to enter into this Agreement.

## 8. Consideration

As consideration for the confirmations, assignment, waiver and covenants in
this Agreement, Erion Beka grants each Confirming Party the recognition stated
on their signature line (if any). The Parties acknowledge that no monetary
payment is required for the validity of this Agreement.

## 9. Further assurances

Each Confirming Party shall, at Erion Beka's request and expense, promptly
execute any further document reasonably necessary to give full effect to this
Agreement and to evidence, record or register the transfer of any rights.

Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the **competent courts in
Prishtina, Republic of Kosovo**. Supplementary matters not regulated by the Law
