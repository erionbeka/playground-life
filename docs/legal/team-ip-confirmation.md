# Team Contribution & IP Confirmation Agreement (Kosovo) — Template

> **Not legal advice.** Have a qualified IP lawyer in Kosovo review this before
> anyone signs. Replace every `[BRACKET]`. Delete this notice before signing.

This is the **long-form** document. Use it when several people were around the
project and you want every one of them on record. It works whether a person
did **some** work (they assign it) or did **none** (they simply confirm that
fact). A version can be signed separately by each person.

---

## Why this document exists

Under **Article 7 of Law No. 08/L-205 on Copyright and Related Rights**
(Official Gazette No. 22/2023, 12 October 2023; in force 27 October 2023), the
**author is the natural person who created the work**. Under **Article 15**,
copyright arises **automatically** on creation — no registration is needed.
Under **Article 9**, joint authorship exists only where there was a
**"joint creative effort"** of two or more authors.

The consequence matters:

- A person who made **no creative contribution** is **not an author** and
  holds **no copyright** to give up. Signing this document does not "take"
  anything from them; it **records the truth** and removes any room for a
  future claim.
- A person who **did** contribute owns something, which this document
  **assigns** to Erion Beka under **Article 37(1)**, so that he becomes the
  new and sole owner.

In both cases the document ends the same way: **Erion Beka is the sole owner.**

## Recitals

(A) Erion Beka created the software, documentation, designs and assets known
as **"Playground Life"** (the "Work").

(B) The Work originated as an idea at the **pristina.innovate.healthcare**
hackathon (04–06 April 2025). The present codebase was created **after** the
event, beginning **April 2026**, and is not a hackathon submission.

(C) Each Confirming Party understands and accepts that authorship of the Work
belongs to the natural person(s) who actually created it, and that under the
Law a person who made no creative contribution is not an author and holds no
copyright in the Work.

(D) To remove all doubt, each Confirming Party wishes to (i) confirm the facts
about what they did or did not do, and (ii) to the extent any right or claim
of theirs exists in the Work, assign and waive it in favour of Erion Beka.

The parties agree as follows.

## 1. Confirmation of facts

1.1 Each Confirming Party confirms that, to the best of their knowledge:

- they did **not** create, write, design or contribute any part of the Work,
  except as individually stated next to their signature and, if any, assigned
  under clause 2; and
- Erion Beka is the person who created the Work.

1.2 Each Confirming Party confirms they were not engaged by Erion Beka (or by
anyone else) as an employee, contractor, consultant or co-author in respect of
the Work, and were not paid or promised anything for it, except as stated in
this Agreement.

1.3 Any specific contribution a Confirming Party believes they made must be
described in writing on the signature page. A contribution that is left blank
is confirmed as **none**.

## 2. Confirmatory assignment (to the extent any rights exist)

2.1 To the extent — and only to the extent — that any Confirming Party owns or
may be found to own any copyright or related economic right in the Work, that
Confirming Party hereby **assigns to Erion Beka**, absolutely and with effect
from the creation of the Work, all such right, title and interest, including
all economic rights (Art. 23), all rights to remuneration, and all rights of
action for past, present and future infringement.

2.2 As a result of such assignment, Erion Beka is the **new and sole owner** of
the rights assigned (Art. 37(1)).

2.2.1 The parties specify the content required by **Article 38(1)** as follows:
nature and extent of use — **all forms of exploitation**; validity period —
the **entire legal term** (author's life plus 70 years, Art. 65(1)); territory
— **worldwide**; remuneration — **EUR 1 (one euro)** where the law requires
consideration, the receipt of which is acknowledged, or **nil** where no
right exists to assign.

2.2 The parties note that, had they left term and territory blank, the Law
would imply a limited scope: territory limited to the Republic of Kosovo
(Art. 38(2)) and a term of only 3 years (original form) or 5 years (adapted
form) (Art. 38(3)). They deliberately choose the broadest lawful scope.

## 3. Existing works only (Art. 39)

This Agreement covers only the **existing, identified Work**. It does not
purport to assign or authorise any right in an indefinite number of
as-yet-uncreated future works, which under **Article 39(1)** would be invalid.

## 4. Moral rights (Arts. 19, 20, 21, 22)

4.1 The parties acknowledge that under **Article 22(1)** moral rights
**cannot be transferred or licensed**, and this Agreement does not attempt to
do so.

4.2 To the extent any Confirming Party holds, or claims, any moral right in
the Work, that Confirming Party:

- **waives**, to the fullest extent permitted by the Law, the right of regret
  under Article 21 and any moral-right claim in respect of the Work; and
- **consents**, for the purposes of **Article 22(2)**, to Erion Beka's use,
  reproduction, modification, distribution and exploitation of the Work.

4.3 Erion Beka's own moral rights (to be recognised and named as the author,
and the right of integrity) remain his and are protected **without limitation
in time** (Art. 64).

## 5. Covenant not to assert

Each Confirming Party **covenants not to assert**, and not to assist any other
person to assert, against Erion Beka, his successors, licensees or customers,
any claim of ownership, authorship, moral right, or compensation in respect of
the Work.

## 6. No admission of wrongdoing

This Agreement is **not** an admission by any party of any wrongdoing. It is a
good-faith record of facts and a settlement of any present or potential doubt
about ownership.

## 7. Warranties

Each Confirming Party warrants that:

1. the facts they confirm in clause 1 are true to the best of their knowledge;
2. if they contribute, the contribution is their own original work, or they
   hold all rights necessary to assign it;
3. they have disclosed any third-party or AI-generated material they are aware
   of that relates to the Work; and
4. they have full capacity and authority to enter into this Agreement.

## 8. Consideration

As consideration for the confirmations, assignment, waiver and covenants in
this Agreement, Erion Beka pays each Confirming Party **EUR [AMOUNT, e.g. 1
or 0]**, the receipt of which each Confirming Party acknowledges; and/or

## 9. Further assurances

Each Confirming Party shall, at Erion Beka's request and expense, promptly
execute any further document reasonably necessary to give full effect to this
Agreement and to evidence, record or register the transfer of any rights.

## 10. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the **laws of the Republic of Kosovo**. The
parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the **competent courts in
Prishtina, Republic of Kosovo**. Supplementary matters not regulated by the
Law are governed by the applicable Kosovo law on obligations (Art. 40(7)).

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the
parties on its subject matter and replaces any prior understanding.

11.2 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the rest remains in

11.3 **Counterparts and electronic signature.** This Agreement may be signed in
