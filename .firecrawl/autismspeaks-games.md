## Virtual activities for kids with autism

While many of these resources offer courses for those with special needs, this is not a list of exclusively autism-friendly programming.

### Learning activities

- [**123 Homeschooling**](https://www.123homeschool4me.com/home-school-free-printables/) – Free homeschooling worksheets.
- [**ABCya!**](https://www.abcya.com/)\- Free educational games organized by grade and subject area.
- [**All Kids Network!**](https://www.allkidsnetwork.com/) – Free resources for children’s crafts, worksheets, coloring pages and more.
- [**BrainPOP**](https://www.brainpop.com/discover) – Educational website with over 1,000 short animated movies for students in grades K-12, together with quizzes and related materials, covering the subjects of science, social studies, English, math, engineering and technology, health, and arts and music.
- [**Boom Cards**](https://wow.boomlearning.com/) \- Premade or customizable flashcards for skill building/maintenance (utilizes discrete-trial training format).
- [**Easton Children's Museum 360**](https://www.cmeaston.org/cme-360/) \- Customizable science programs.
- [**Crash Course**](https://thecrashcourse.com/) – Engaging educational videos suitable for _high school students_. The channel features a wide range of subjects, from anatomy to world history.
- [**Highlights Kids**](https://www.highlightskids.com/) – Online magazine with activities, games, recipes.
- [**Kids Activities Blog**](https://kidsactivitiesblog.com/135609/list-of-education-companies-offering-free-subscriptions/) – List of free learning websites for kids.
- [**KidsKonnect**](https://kidskonnect.com/) - Printable worksheets for teachers & homeschool parents.
- [**Math Worksheets 4 Kids**](https://www.mathworksheets4kids.com/) \- Provides Language Arts, Math, Science, and Social studies printable materials.
- [**The Math Worksheet Site**](https://www.mathworksheets4kids.com/) \- Provides Mathematics-related printable materials.
- [**Mystery Science**](https://mysteryscience.com/) \- Hands-on science lessons.
- [**Mommy Poppins Guide to Science Experiments**](https://mommypoppins.com/family/stem/guide-to-science-experiments-for-kids-and-fun-stem-activities) – Science experiments and STEM activities for kids.
- [**PBS SoCal, KCET, and PBS**](https://www.pbssocal.org/about-at-home-learning) – Free, standards-aligned videos, interactives, lesson plans for at-home learning.
- [**Originator**](https://www.originatorkids.com/) – List of educational mobile and tablet applications for children and families. Fees apply.
- [**Reading A-Z**](https://www.readinga-z.com/) **,** [**RAZ kids**](https://www.raz-kids.com/) **and** [**Vocabulary A-Z**](https://www.vocabularya-z.com/#!/)\- Provides leveled books, passages, comprehension printable materials, quizzes for interactive reading practice and vocabulary word lists/lessons with online practice. Subscription.
- [**Scholastic Learn at Home**](https://www.scholastic.com/parents/school-success/learn-at-home.html) – Allows open access to daily learning journeys divided into four grade spans: _**Pre-K – K**_, _**Grades 1-2**_, _**Grades 3-5**_, and _**Grades 6-9+**_, covering ELA, STEAM, science, social studies, and social-emotional learning. Free.
- [**Starfall**](https://www.starfall.com/h/) \- Provides Reading and Math games, movies, books and music.
- [**Universal Class**](https://www.universalclass.com/i/librarycourse/learn_how_to_make_unit_studies.htm) – Classes to guide parents and teachers to build their own unit study courses for homeschool purposes. Free access with a Los Angeles Public Library card (which can now be obtained online).
- [**www.EdHelper.com**](https://www.edhelper.com/) \- Provides Reading, Grammar, Math and Science printable materials. Subscription
- [**www.Education.com**](https://www.education.com/) \- Provides Reading, Writing, Math, Science, Social Studies printable materials as well as Reading and Math online games. Subscription
- [**www.EnchantedLearning.com**](https://www.enchantedlearning.com/) \- provides Writing and Science printable materials. Subscription
- [**www.mathworksheetsland.com**](https://www.mathworksheetsland.com/) \- provides Mathematics-related printable materials. Subscription
- [**Science at Home with Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute**](https://www.mbari.org/education/more-resources/science-at-home/) – Collection of educational science resources from the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute.

- [**Autism Speaks DIY Sensory Bottle**](https://www.autismspeaks.org/blog/video-diy-sensory-bottles) \- Create a DIY sensory bottle for yourself or a sensory-seeker in your family.
- [**Autism Speaks DIY Stress Creatures**](https://www.autismspeaks.org/blog/video-diy-stress-creatures) – Create fun stress-relieving fidget characters.
- **Neurabilities** [**Make Your Own Instrument Crafts**](https://www.facebook.com/NeurAbilities/videos/1148849215451927/)
- **Kadiant** [**Sensory Fun: Rice Bins**](https://youtu.be/bNQWsu0VP9o?si=orS-gPJbgHemgqO3)
- [**Elise Gravel**](https://elisegravel.com/en/livres/free-printables/) – Free, printable coloring pages by children’s author and illustrator.
- [**Google Arts & Culture**](https://artsandculture.google.com/) – Guide to museums and galleries offering virtual exhibits and tours.
- [**Khan Academy**](https://www.khanacademy.org/) – Walt Disney Imagineering partners with Khan Academy for Imagineering in a Box. The program is designed to pull back the curtain to show you how artists, designers, and engineers work together to create theme parks. Go behind the scenes with Disney Imagineers and complete project-based exercise to design a theme park of your very own.
- [**Lunch Doodles with Mo Willems**](https://youtu.be/RmzjCPQv3y8?si=zbRZiVcIGtstikHa) – Children’s author, Mo Williams welcomes you into his study at home and guides you through drawing activities using one of his favorite characters for inspiration.
- [**Louvre**](https://www.louvre.fr/en/online-tours)– Visit the museum’s exhibition rooms and galleries.
- [**National History Museum of Los Angeles County**](https://nhmlac.org/make-your-own-dinosaur-shadow-puppet-and-screen) – Make Your Own Dinosaur Shadow Puppet and Screen.

### Outdoors and animals

- [**American Museum of Natural History**](https://www.amnh.org/explore/ology) – Their Ology science website provides games and activities for kids and covers a range of science topics, including archaeology, paleontology, astronomy, and marine biology.
- [**Center for Discovery**](https://thecenterfordiscovery.org/blog/safe-indoor-and-outdoor-activities-for-families-in-the-time-of-coronavirus/) – Safe indoor and outdoor activities for families in the time of the coronavirus.
- [**Explore Mars from NASA**](https://accessmars.withgoogle.com/) – Explore the real surface of Mars, recorded by Nasa’s Curiosity rover.
- [**Google Arts & Culture and National Parks**](https://artsandculture.google.com/project/national-park-service) – Google has teamed up with five national parks in the US so people can enjoy the beauty of the natural world virtually: Kenai Fjords National Park (Alaska), Hawai’i Volcanoes National Park (Hawaii), Carlsbad Caverns National Park (New Mexico), Bryce Canyon National Park (Utah), Dry Tortugas National Park (Florida).
- [**Live Science**](https://www.livescience.com/) – Breaks down stories behind the most interesting news and photos on the Internet, while also digging up fascinating discoveries that hit on a broad range of fields, from dinosaurs and archaeology to wacky physics and astronomy to health and human behavior.
- [**Los Angeles Zoo**](https://lazoo.org/) – Videos of animals.
- [**National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA)**](https://sanctuaries.noaa.gov/vr/) \- Tour marine sanctuaries and ocean habitats with a collection of virtual dives from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.
- [**San Diego Zoo Wildlife Explorer**](https://sdzwildlifeexplorers.org/) – Videos, activities, and games from the San Diego Zoo.
- [**Smithsonian’s National Zoo**](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/webcams) – Animal webcams at the zoo.
- [**The Nature Museum in Chicago**](https://naturemuseum.org/chicago-academy-of-sciences-blog) – How do butterflies eat? What’s the difference between frogs and toads? The Nature Museum in Chicago has videos and activities to acquaint kids with the natural world and to inspire their curiosity about the plants and animals in their own backyards.
- [**Yellowstone National Park**](https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/photosmultimedia/virtualtours.htm) – Virtual tours of some of Yellowstone National Park’s main attractions around the park.

### Reading

- [**Audible**](https://www.audible.com/) – Free audiobooks for kids and teens.
- [**CommonLit**](https://www.commonlit.org/) – Free reading passages and literacy resources in all literary and nonfiction genres for _**Grades 3-12**_.
- [**PJ Library**](https://pjlibrary.org/enroll-in-a-community) \- sign up to receive free books and participate in virtual storytimes.
- [**Storytime at Los Angeles Public Library**](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLh7t-m6ncxjUIhLga1skZ7y3rXJeOxeqL) – Recorded storytime with the Los Angeles Public Library staff. There are also guided storytimes so that families can create their own version at home.
- [**Time for Kids**](https://www.timeforkids.com/) **–** For _**elementary**_ and _**middle school students**_, digital subscriptions are free for the duration of the school year. This is the first time that the magazine is fully accessible at home.
- [**Tumblebook**](https://www.tumblebooklibrary.com/default.aspx) – An online collection of animated, talking picture books, which teach young children the joys of reading. Books available in seven different languages. Free trail available.

### Technology

- [**Scratch (MIT Media Lab)**](https://scratch.mit.edu/about) – Learn to code for ages 8-16, but used by people of all ages. Program your own interactive stories, games, and animation and share with others in the online community. Scratch helps young people learn to think creatively, reason systematically, and work collaboratively.
