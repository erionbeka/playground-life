# Game Night is Online!

Meet and play online from anywhere in the world

PlayingCards.io provides online rooms to play games, learn, and hang out

## Create your room

Create a private room for your group by selecting a game on the [Games Page](https://playingcards.io/games) and clicking the Start button. Then, have the others join by entering your room code on PlayingCards.io.

## Virtual tabletop

Drag objects in your room to play, all movements are synchronized live between all players. It's up to you to enforce the rules and taking turns, just like a real card table.

animation

## Bring your own chat

You'll want to be talking with the other players during the game. Use another site or the good ol' telephone to chat.
